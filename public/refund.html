Refund Policy

How refunds, delivery problems and billing issues are handled for a personalized digital service.

Last updated: August 14, 2026.

Paddle handles the transaction

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for purchases completed through Paddle Checkout. Refunds and payment reversals are processed by Paddle, not by HairLook AI directly. Paddle's buyer terms, refund policy and applicable consumer law govern the final payment remedy.

If your order is not delivered

If a verified paid order is not delivered to your verified email within the stated 72-hour period, contact HairLook AI support with the email used for the order and your transaction reference if available. We will investigate promptly and, where appropriate, complete the service, re-perform the service, or coordinate an appropriate refund/remedy through Paddle.

If there is a material product problem

If the delivered service is materially different from the product description or a persistent technical issue prevents you from receiving or using the delivered results, contact us first so we can attempt to resolve the issue. If it cannot reasonably be resolved, we will cooperate with Paddle's refund process and applicable consumer law.

Personalized digital results

HairLook AI is a personalized digital service. A preference for a different hairstyle direction by itself does not automatically mean that the service is defective. However, this does not limit any mandatory rights you may have where a product is not as described, faulty or otherwise legally refundable.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact us and/or use the support options shown in your Paddle receipt. Never email full payment-card numbers or security codes.

How to request a refund

Start by emailing support@example.com with the purchase email and order reference. You may also use the buyer-support/refund links provided by Paddle in your transaction receipt. Paddle makes the final payment refund to the original payment method where applicable.

Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy removes withdrawal, refund or consumer-protection rights that apply in your jurisdiction. Where local law provides stronger rights, those rights take priority.
