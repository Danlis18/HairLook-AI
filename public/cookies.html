Cookie & Storage Notice

How HairLook AI uses essential cookies and browser storage to keep the consultation and private order flow working.

Last updated: August 14, 2026.

Essential session cookies

HairLook AI uses secure, HTTP-only session cookies to keep a verified customer connected to their private order and to keep administrators signed in to protected administrative tools. These cookies are necessary for security and core service operation.

Session and local browser storage

The consultation may use browser session storage to remember quiz progress, anonymous session identifiers and campaign attribution while you move through the flow. This prevents accidental loss of progress and helps us understand basic first-party funnel performance.

Analytics

We record first-party product events such as landing views, quiz progress, photo upload completion, email verification and checkout start. We do not put uploaded photo contents or payment-card data into analytics metadata.

Payment provider

When you open Paddle Checkout, Paddle may use cookies or similar technologies required to provide checkout, prevent fraud, remember payment preferences and comply with legal obligations. Paddle handles these under its own privacy and cookie practices.

Advertising cookies

The current HairLook AI implementation does not intentionally set third-party advertising or retargeting cookies on the main consultation flow. If this changes, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated before deployment.

Your controls

You can clear browser storage and cookies using your browser settings. Removing essential session data may sign you out or require you to restart parts of the consultation.

Contact

Questions about cookies or browser storage can be sent to support@example.com.
