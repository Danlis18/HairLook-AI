About

A calmer way to consider a hair change.

HairLook AI is a private digital hairstyle consultation designed to help adults explore useful visual directions before a real-world salon decision.

What the service does

You complete a guided consultation about length, texture, color, maintenance and style direction, upload one clear portrait, verify your email and place a one-time order. We then prepare personalized hairstyle visualizations tied to your choices and deliver them to your verified email within 72 hours.

What the service does not claim

We do not market HairLook AI as a medical analysis, biometric identification service, professional hair diagnosis or guarantee of an identical salon outcome. The results are visual planning material intended to make a hairstyle conversation easier.

Current fulfillment model

The current production workflow is deliberately human-supervised. Paid orders are reviewed through a protected admin panel and prepared for private email delivery. Automated generation and automated final-result email delivery may be added later only after the workflow, quality controls and privacy disclosures are updated accordingly.

Privacy and security

Uploaded portraits are kept in private storage and accessed through short-lived server-mediated links. Customer email verification happens before checkout, and payment status is accepted only after verified server-side notification from Paddle.

Who we build for

The experience is designed for adults who want a clear, polished and private way to explore a new haircut or color direction without pressure, exaggerated promises or hidden subscriptions.
