About

A calmer way to consider a hair change.

HairLook AI is designed as a private digital consultation before a salon appointment — not a feed of random AI images.

What we are building

One clear photo and a guided style profile become a curated set of visual directions: practical lengths, modern layers, easy-care options, believable color changes, gray-friendly ideas and a few bolder comparisons.

What we are not claiming

We do not present a fake facial scan, a medical diagnosis or a guarantee that AI can predict an exact salon outcome. The purpose is decision confidence: helping a person see useful directions before committing to a real cut or color.

Private by design

The production architecture keeps original portraits and generated results in private storage, uses short-lived URLs, server-side payment verification and a separate generation worker. The operator can set clear retention periods and customers can request deletion.

Our audience

The experience is built to feel clear and sophisticated for adults who value confidence, privacy and usability — including women 45+ — without turning age into a stereotype or a marketing claim.
