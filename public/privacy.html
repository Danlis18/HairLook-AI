Privacy Policy

How HairLook AI handles quiz answers, verified email addresses, uploaded portraits and order information.

Last updated: August 14, 2026. This policy applies to HairLook AI, operated by HairLook AI, .

Information we collect

We collect information you intentionally submit during the consultation, including email address, age range, current and desired hair length, texture, color preferences, style goals, maintenance preferences, bangs and gray preferences. We also process the portrait you upload, email-verification status, order/payment status, basic security/session information, and first-party campaign attribution such as UTM parameters.

How we use your information

We use this information to save your consultation, verify your email, prepare and deliver the personalized hairstyle service, provide order support, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain service security, understand first-party funnel performance and comply with legal/payment obligations.

How your photo is handled

Your portrait is stored in private storage and is not intentionally published as a public asset. In the current manual-fulfillment workflow, authorized operators may access a short-lived private link in the admin area to download the portrait only for preparing and supporting your paid hairstyle order. If automated image providers are enabled in the future, this policy should be updated before those providers process customer images.

No biometric identification

The current service does not intentionally create or retain facial-recognition embeddings, biometric identity templates or medical diagnoses from uploaded portraits. Hairstyle results are visual planning material, not identity verification or health analysis.

Email verification and delivery

We send a six-digit verification code to the email you provide before checkout. Order-related messages and final hairstyle results are delivered to the verified email tied to the order. Transactional email delivery is currently provided through Resend.

Payments

Paddle acts as Merchant of Record for purchases completed through Paddle Checkout. Paddle processes billing and payment information under Paddle's own terms and privacy notice. HairLook AI does not store full payment-card numbers.

Storage and retention

Uploaded originals are currently configured for retention of approximately 720 hours unless earlier deletion is requested or operational/legal needs require otherwise. Result files stored by HairLook AI are currently configured for approximately 30 days. Payment, fraud, security, support and legal/audit records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary or required by law.

Service providers

We use service providers for hosting, database/storage, transactional email and payments. Current infrastructure includes Railway for application hosting, Supabase for database/private storage, Resend for transactional email, and Paddle for checkout/payment services. Each provider processes data under its own contractual and privacy obligations.

Security

Private files are accessed through server-mediated operations and short-lived signed URLs. Browser clients do not receive Supabase secret keys, Paddle webhook secrets or Resend API keys. Administrative access is protected and sensitive operations are logged.

Analytics and browser storage

We record first-party funnel events such as landing view, quiz progression, upload completion, email verification and checkout start. We do not intentionally put raw photo content or full payment-card data into analytics metadata. See our Cookie & Storage Notice for details about essential session cookies and browser session storage.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. You may also request deletion of remaining private HairLook AI data where applicable. Mandatory rights are not limited by this policy.

Children

The service is intended for adults and is not designed for children. Do not upload a child's portrait unless the operator has separately implemented the consent and legal safeguards required for that use case.
