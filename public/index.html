Frequently asked

Questions before you upload.

What exactly am I buying?

A one-time personalized digital hairstyle visualization service. The current standard price is $6.99 USD. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. See the Product Details page for the full description.

Will the previews still look like me?

Your hairstyle previews are prepared from your uploaded portrait and reviewed as hairstyle directions, not as a guarantee of an identical real-world salon outcome.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is stored privately for the time needed to fulfill your hairstyle order. Access is server-mediated, and private originals are exposed to authorized operators only through short-lived signed links.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Your results are delivered to the email tied to your order. You enter the address once, then we immediately send a 6-digit code so you can confirm it before checkout.

How long will my results take?

After your payment is confirmed, your personalized hairstyle results are prepared and sent to your verified email.

How is payment confirmed?

Payment status is accepted only after server-side verification. Available payment methods are shown before you pay.

Can the collection include gray-friendly ideas?

Yes. The prepared collection can include natural gray, soft gray blending, coverage or several directions.
