Frequently asked

Questions before you upload.

Will the previews still look like me?

The generation prompt is built to preserve the same identity, apparent age, skin tone, pose and background while editing the hairstyle and color. AI can still make imperfect edits, which is why the collection gives you multiple directions rather than presenting one image as a guarantee.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is stored privately for the time needed to generate your results. The default original-photo retention is configurable and the live policy is shown before upload. Result images are also private and are delivered through short-lived access links.

Is this a subscription?

No. The standard offer is a one-time purchase. The secure payment page shows the authoritative charge before you pay.

Can I explore gray-friendly options?

Yes. The consultation includes keeping natural gray, soft gray blending, covering gray, or seeing several options.

Do you automatically “scan” my face shape?

No fake scan is shown. The product is preference-led. If face proportions are ever used automatically in the future, that feature should be introduced only with clear disclosure and appropriate privacy review.

Can I access my results later?

Yes. Use the email address from your order to request a secure one-time sign-in link. Result retention is disclosed in the privacy policy and can be configured by the operator.
